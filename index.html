01 — The Gym

A gym that fits into a Bahria Town day.

Life Fitness Gym sits in the basement of Plot 129, in the Civic Center of Bahria Town Phase 4 — next to KFC, near Reliance Hospital, right where the neighbourhood already passes through. It is a working gym, not a showpiece: racks, benches, machines and a cardio row, with trainers on the floor while members train.

Members come from the blocks around the Civic Center — before work, after college, in the evening after the shops close. The routine is simple: turn up, train, go home stronger than yesterday.

02 — Training

Four ways to work.

No single programme fits everyone. The floor is set up so you can train the way your week needs — heavy, steady, guided, or all three.

Chapter 01

Strength

Barbells, dumbbells and plate machines for building strength set by set. Lift with a spotter nearby and a trainer on the floor when your form needs a second pair of eyes.

Chapter 02

Cardio

Treadmills and a cardio row for warming up, cooling down, or a full session of steady work. Walk, jog or run at your own pace, for as long as your plan calls for.

Chapter 03

Free Weights & Functional

Open floor space, racks and functional kit for the work machines can’t do — carries, presses, squats and the movements that keep everyday life easy.

Chapter 04

Coaching

Trainers are on the floor while you train. Ask for a spot, a form check, or a plan for the week — that is what they are there for.

03 — The Floor

The basics, done well.

Everything on the floor earns its place: the racks you reach for every session, the machines that isolate a muscle, and the open space to move in.

  • Strength floor Benches, racks and plate machines
  • Cardio row Treadmills for warm-ups and steady sessions
  • Free-weights area Dumbbells from light to heavy, racked in order
  • Functional corner Open space, medicine balls and plates
  • Stretching space Room to warm up and cool down properly

For everyone

First-timers, regulars, and people starting again.

Members train at every level here — people on their first week, people who have trained for years, and people coming back after a long break. Nobody is expected to arrive already fit. That is what the gym is for.

Come as you are, train at your own pace, and ask the trainers when you are unsure. The floor is shared, the equipment is shared, and the progress is your own.

Questions

Asked before you ask.

Where exactly is the gym?

In the basement of Plot 129, Civic Center, Bahria Town Phase 4 — near KFC and Reliance Hospital. If you are driving in, head for the Civic Center and ask for Plot 129.

How do I join?

Call 0345 8167919 or visit the gym. Membership rates are confirmed at the gym — prices on request.

Are there trainers on the floor?

Yes — trainers are on the floor while members train, for spotting, form checks and planning your week.

What are the opening hours?

Hours are confirmed by phone, so call 0345 8167919 to check today’s timings before your first visit.

I have never trained before. Is this gym for me?

Yes. Start with a visit, walk the floor, and talk to a trainer about a simple first plan. Nobody is expected to arrive already fit.

What should I bring to my first session?

Comfortable training clothes, indoor training shoes, a towel and a water bottle. Everything else is on the floor.
